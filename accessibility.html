Accessibility Statement

Last updated: August 2026

AI Doctor Study is committed to ensuring digital accessibility for people with disabilities. We continually improve the user experience for everyone and apply relevant accessibility standards.

Measures We Take

  • Include accessibility as part of our mission and values
  • Use semantic HTML elements throughout the application
  • Provide sufficient colour contrast for text and UI elements
  • Support keyboard navigation where possible
  • Respect the user's preference for reduced motion
  • Provide text alternatives for non-text content

Conformance Status

AI Doctor Study is partially conformant with WCAG 2.1 level AA. Partially conformant means that some parts of the content do not fully conform to the accessibility standard.

Known Limitations

  • Some AI-generated content may not have structured headings
  • Audio transcription features require microphone access
  • Some interactive flashcard animations may be reduced in prefers-reduced-motion mode

Feedback

We welcome your feedback on the accessibility of AI Doctor Study. Please contact us at aidoctorstudy@gmail.com if you experience barriers.

Assessment Approach

AI Doctor Study assessed the accessibility of this application by self-evaluation.
