Cookie Policy

Last updated: 17 August 2026 · Applies to aidoctor.study and the AI Doctor Study mobile app

The short version

  • We use a small set of cookies and local storage to keep you signed in and remember your study profile — those are essential and always on.
  • Analytics only runs if you say yes in the cookie banner. Say no and the app works exactly the same.
  • Gumroad (payments) and YouTube (videos) may set their own cookies when you use those features.
  • You can change your mind anytime via Cookie settings or your browser.

01What cookies & local storage are

Cookies are small text files a website stores on your device so it can recognise you between pages and visits. Local storage is a similar browser feature we use to save data (like your study profile) directly on your device instead of on our servers. This policy covers both, plus similar technologies like SDK identifiers in our mobile app.

02How we use them

  • Essential — signing you in with Google (Firebase Authentication), keeping your session secure, and remembering your cookie choice. The Service can't work without these, so they don't require consent.
  • Functional — remembering your name, study year, and exam so you land straight in your study space instead of re-doing onboarding.
  • Analytics (optional) — anonymous-style usage statistics via Google Analytics / Firebase Analytics, so we can see which features help students and which need fixing. These run only with your consent.

We don't use advertising cookies, and we don't sell your data.

04Third-party cookies

  • Gumroad — when you open the Pro checkout or manage your subscription, Gumroad sets its own cookies to process your payment securely. See Gumroad's Privacy Policy.
  • YouTube — some study videos are embedded from YouTube. When you play one, YouTube (Google) may set cookies and collect data under the Google Privacy Policy.

These third parties control their own cookies — we can't switch them off from our side, but they only load when you use those features.

05Your choices

The cookie banner. On your first visit you can choose Accept all or Essential only. Analytics stays off unless you accept. Your choice is saved on your device and you can change it anytime:

Your browser. You can also block or delete cookies and site data in your browser settings — note that deleting essential storage will sign you out and reset your on-device study profile.

Google's opt-out. For Google Analytics specifically, Google offers a browser opt-out add-on.

06Changes to this policy

If we add new cookies or change how we use them, we'll update this page and, where the change is significant, ask for your consent again through the banner. The "Last updated" date above shows the current version.
