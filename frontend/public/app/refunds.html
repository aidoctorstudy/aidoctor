Refund Policy

Last updated: 17 August 2026 · Applies to AI Doctor Study Pro purchases made through Gumroad

The short version

  • First month didn't work out? Email us within 7 days of your first Pro purchase and we'll refund it, no drama.
  • Cancelling ≠ refund. Cancel anytime — you keep Pro until your paid month ends, and you just won't be charged again.
  • Renewals generally aren't refundable once a new month starts, except for billing mistakes or where the law says otherwise.
  • Refunds go back to your original payment method via Gumroad, usually within 5–10 business days.
  • Once a payment is refunded or charged back, Pro access ends immediately.

01How billing works

AI Doctor Study Pro is a monthly subscription billed through Gumroad, our payment provider and merchant of record. Gumroad charges your payment method, issues your receipt, and handles any applicable taxes. Refunds we approve are issued through Gumroad back to the payment method you used.

02Cancelling your subscription

You can cancel anytime — no notice period, no questions:

  1. Open the app → Settings → Subscription → Cancel Subscription (this takes you to Gumroad, where the cancellation is completed), or
  2. Cancel directly from your Gumroad subscriptions page or the link in your Gumroad receipt email.

Cancelling stops all future charges. You keep full Pro access until the end of the period you've already paid for, then your account moves back to the free plan automatically. Cancelling is not a refund of the current period, and we don't give partial refunds for unused days.

037-day guarantee on your first purchase

We want Pro to actually help you study. If it's not for you, email us within 7 days of your first Pro payment and we'll refund it in full. This applies once per person, to your first payment only — it's a chance to try Pro properly, not a recurring free month.

04Renewal payments

Because you can cancel anytime before a renewal, renewal payments are generally non-refundable once the new billing period has started. We make exceptions when something on our side went wrong, for example:

  • you were charged after cancelling before the renewal date;
  • you were charged twice for the same period;
  • a serious technical problem on our side made Pro unusable for a substantial part of the period and we couldn't fix it.

If any of those happen, contact us and we'll make it right — with a refund or account credit for the affected period.

05How to request a refund

Email support@aidoctor.study from any address, and include:

  • the email address on your Gumroad receipt;
  • the approximate date of the charge (or the Gumroad order/receipt number if you have it);
  • a sentence on what went wrong — it genuinely helps us improve the app.

We aim to reply within 2 business days. Approved refunds are issued through Gumroad and typically appear on your statement within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank.

06What happens after a refund

When a payment is refunded, the Pro access it paid for ends immediately — your account returns to the free plan and your study data stays intact. If a refunded charge is somehow processed again by mistake, contact us and we'll reverse it.

07Chargebacks

Please talk to us first

Chargebacks (disputing a charge with your bank) cost small products like ours real money in fees, even when resolved. If anything looks wrong on your statement, email us before disputing — we can almost always fix it faster than your bank can.

When a chargeback is filed, Gumroad notifies us and Pro access is revoked automatically and immediately. We may decline future purchases from accounts with abusive chargeback activity. Of course, nothing here limits your legal right to dispute genuinely unauthorised charges.

08Your statutory rights

This policy sits alongside your rights as a consumer — it never reduces them. If the consumer-protection law where you live gives you stronger refund or cancellation rights (for example, cooling-off rights for digital subscriptions in some countries), those rights apply. Where local law requires it, by starting your subscription immediately you consent to immediate supply of the digital service and acknowledge how this may affect any statutory withdrawal right.

09Changes to this policy

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. The version in force when you were charged is the one that applies to that charge. The "Last updated" date above shows the current version.

10Contact

Anything unclear, or a situation this page doesn't cover? Email support@aidoctor.study — a human reads it, and we'd rather sort it out than argue about it.
