Privacy Policy

AI Doctor Study — aidoctor.study

The short version: We collect only what we need to run the app. We never sell your data. We never share it with advertisers. You can delete your account anytime.

1. Who We Are

AI Doctor Study is an AI-powered medical education platform built for medical students, based in the United Arab Emirates. Contact us at aidoctorstudy@gmail.com.

2. What Data We Collect

Account Information
  • Email address (required to create an account)
  • Display name (you choose this)
  • Year of study and exam type (MBBS, USMLE, PLAB etc)
  • If you sign in with Google: your Google profile name and email
Usage Data
  • XP points and streak count (stored locally in your browser)
  • Study preferences and settings
  • Flashcards and notes you generate (stored in your browser, not our servers)
Payment Data (Pro subscribers only)
  • Your email address (to verify Pro status)
  • Payment is processed entirely by Gumroad — we never see or store your card details
Technical Data
  • IP address (used only for rate limiting — not stored long term)
  • Browser type and device type (for debugging only)
  • Error logs (anonymous, no personal data)

3. What We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not store the content of your AI chat conversations on our servers
  • We do not use tracking pixels or advertising cookies
  • We do not build advertising profiles
  • We do not collect your location
  • We do not sell your data to anyone, ever

4. How We Use Your Data

  • To create and manage your account
  • To verify your Pro subscription status
  • To send important account emails (password reset etc)
  • To prevent abuse of our AI features (rate limiting by IP)
  • To improve the app based on anonymous usage patterns

5. AI and Your Content

When you use AI features, your input is sent to our AI providers:

  • Groq (text and audio) — Groq Privacy Policy
  • Moonshot AI / Kimi via OpenRouter (image analysis) — OpenRouter Privacy Policy

We do not store your AI conversations or audio recordings on our servers. Inputs are processed in real time and not retained.

6. Data Storage and Security

  • Account data is stored in Google Firebase (US servers) — Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Your flashcards, XP and study progress are stored locally in your browser — we cannot access this
  • All connections are encrypted via HTTPS/TLS
  • We use rate limiting and input sanitization to protect the platform

7. Cookies

We use only essential cookies required for authentication (Firebase Auth session cookies). We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, delete or export your data. Email us at aidoctorstudy@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

9. Data Retention

  • Account data: kept while your account is active, deleted within 30 days of deletion request
  • Pro subscription records: kept for 7 years for financial compliance
  • Server logs: automatically deleted after 30 days
  • IP rate limit data: deleted after 1 hour automatically

10. Children

AI Doctor Study is designed for medical students typically 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. Contact us immediately if you believe a child under 13 has an account.

11. Third Party Services

  • Google Firebase — authentication and database
  • Vercel — website hosting
  • Cloudflare — security, CDN and AI proxy
  • Gumroad — payment processing
  • Groq — AI text and audio processing
  • OpenRouter / Moonshot AI — AI image analysis

12. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by posting a notice on the app. Continued use after changes means you accept the new policy.

13. Contact Us

  • Email: aidoctorstudy@gmail.com
  • Website: aidoctor.study
